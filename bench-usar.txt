EXPERIMENT 3 - hop-optimal vs distance-optimal
==========================================================================
pairs where the fewest-hop route is also the shortest: 49
pairs where the fewest-hop route walks further:          17

worst case: Block B (USDI) -> Girls' Hostel
  fewest hops : 595 m
  shortest    : 275 m  (320 m / 116% less)

conclusion: on a weighted campus graph, minimising turns and
minimising metres are different objectives. Dijkstra is the
correct algorithm for walking distance; BFS is used for the
unweighted comparison the task asks for.
