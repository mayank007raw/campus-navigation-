The map, before and after

Both maps are drawn by the same code from the same algorithms. The only thing that changed is where the data came from.
Before — invented

14 buildings with plausible names, invented coordinates, invented distances — including a node called “USAR Building”. A reviewer has no way to check a single number, and anyone who knows the campus can tell immediately.

Now — the real campus

Laid out from your satellite screenshot and OpenStreetMap building coordinates. Every point declares whether it is a surveyed OSM feature or a pixel measurement from the screenshot, and the remaining gaps are listed rather than filled in with guesses.
