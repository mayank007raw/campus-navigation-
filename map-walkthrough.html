The two routes worth quoting

Block A (USAR) → Block B (USDI)

BFS says 2 hops, 300 m, out by the gate.
Dijkstra says 2 hops, 110 m, past the Library.
Same number of turnings. 63% less walking. Dijkstra's answer is the route the student described.

Two independent sources agree on the order

A campus tour video, filmed by a student and read as a transcript, gives the order sports hall → main building → auditorium → ground and hostel.
That is the order the student's corrections produced, before the video was found. Agreement between two people who have walked it is the closest thing to verification this map has.
